Features

Everything you need to sideload, on the device.

Catalyst keeps everything SideStore does and adds its own tools for signing, customizing and managing your apps.

Signing & refreshing

Apple ID signing

Sign in with any Apple ID, free or paid, and install apps straight from your sources.

Enterprise signing

Import your own .p12 and .mobileprovision and sign apps without an Apple ID.

No 7-day refresh

Enterprise-signed apps show SIGNED instead of a countdown and last until the profile expires.

No 3-app limit

Install as many Enterprise-signed apps as you like. Apple ID apps keep the usual limit.

Background refresh

Apple ID apps refresh themselves before they expire, with optional Cellular Refresh through Shortcuts.

Choose per install

Pick a signing method once, or have Catalyst ask each time you install an app.

Customize

Customizable entitlements

Review, add or remove an app's entitlements before it's signed.

Custom bundle identifiers

Change an app's bundle ID, for example to install a second copy next to the first.

Edit Info.plist

Add or change Info.plist keys for any app you install.

Custom app icons

Give an app a different Home Screen icon when you install it.

App Groups

Create App Groups on your team and assign them to your apps.

Themes

Black with a touch of purple by default, with a Theme Manager to make it yours.

Manage & tools

On-device pairing

Create this device's pairing file inside Catalyst. No computer, on iOS 27 or later.

Sources & News

Add any AltStore-format source to browse apps, get updates and read news.

Developer portal

See and manage the App IDs, devices, certificates and profiles on your team.

JIT

Enable JIT for apps that need it, such as emulators, through SideJITServer.

Backup & Restore

Back up an app's data before you remove or reinstall it, and restore it later.

Works without an App Group

Catalyst still starts when a signer strips its App Group. Only widgets are unavailable then.

Setup

From download to your first install.

Whichever certificate you use, installing apps needs a pairing file for your device and LocalDevVPN.

Install Catalyst

Download the IPA and install it with the sideloading tool you already use, or with your Enterprise certificate.

Pair this device

In Settings › Pairing & Signing › Generate Pairing File, tap Start Pairing. Then open Developer Mode in iOS Settings, tap Pair with Catalyst and enter the code.

Turn on LocalDevVPN

Connect LocalDevVPN, choose a signing method and install apps from Browse or from an IPA file.

Catalyst never ships, downloads or shares certificates. Only import a certificate your organization issued to you. Apple revokes Enterprise certificates that are shared publicly, and every app signed with a revoked certificate stops opening.

FAQ

Questions

Is Catalyst free?

Yes. Catalyst is free and open source under the GNU AGPLv3.

Do I need a computer?

No. Catalyst pairs with your device and installs apps on the device itself. On iOS 27 or later it can create the pairing file too; it needs Developer Mode on and LocalDevVPN connected.

Where do I get an Enterprise certificate?

From the organization you belong to. Catalyst doesn't provide certificates and never will. Using someone else's certificate breaks Apple's terms and gets it revoked.

Can I change an app's entitlements or bundle ID?

Yes. Before an app is signed, Catalyst lets you change its bundle identifier, entitlements, Info.plist keys and icon. That's handy for installing two copies of the same app or turning off a feature you don't need.

Why do Apple ID apps need refreshing?

With a free Apple ID, apps are signed for 7 days and you can have 3 active at once. Catalyst refreshes them in the background, the same way SideStore does. Enterprise-signed apps don't have this limit.

How is Catalyst related to SideStore?

Catalyst is a fork of SideStore, which is based on AltStore. It keeps their features and adds Enterprise signing, on-device pairing and its own look. It isn't affiliated with SideStore, AltStore or Apple.
